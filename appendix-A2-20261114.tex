\section*{A.2. FISTA Implementation and Control Flow}

\renewcommand{\thealgorithm}{A\arabic{algorithm}}

All dwell times in the initial catheter pool were initialized to 1 s so that all catheters were initially active. FISTA used groupwise proximal updates with backtracking. The first block ran for 500 iterations before $K$ was updated. Each subsequent block began with 50 stabilization iterations without convergence assessment. After this period, 10 consecutive iterations ending in line-search failure triggered an early $K$ update and initiated the next block.

3 & \hspace*{6.475pt}Set sparsity weights using Equation (5) & \# Assign catheter penalties \\
4 & \hspace*{6.475pt}\textbf{For} $k\  = \ 1,\ \ldots,\ 20,000$: & \# Limit FISTA iterations \\
5 & \hspace*{15.125pt}$t\  \leftarrow \ FISTAUpdate\left( t,\ K \right)$ & \# Minimize $F_{\text{dose}}\left( t \right)\  + \ F_{\text{sparse}}\left( t \right)$ \\
6 & \hspace*{15.125pt}$\mathcal{C}\  \leftarrow \ ActiveCatheters\left( t \right)$ & \# Identify retained catheters \\
7 & \hspace*{15.125pt}\textbf{If} $\text{CoverageCheckDue}\left( k\mathcal{,\ C},\ Anchor \right)$: & \# First check or count reduction \\
8 & \hspace*{23.75pt}$t^{\text{check}}\  \leftarrow \ DoseReopt\left( \mathcal{C,\ }t \right)$ & \# Assess dose-only feasibility \\
9 & \hspace*{23.75pt}\textbf{If} $D_{90}\left( t^{\text{check}} \right)\  \geq \ 0.95D_{T}^{\text{LB}}$: & \# Check target coverage \\
10 & \hspace*{32.4pt}$Anchor\  \leftarrow \ \left( \mathcal{C,\ }t \right)$ & \# Save the FISTA solution \\
11 & \hspace*{23.75pt}\textbf{Else if} $Anchor\  = \ \varnothing$: & \# No feasible anchor available \\
12 & \hspace*{32.4pt}\textbf{Return} FAILURE & \# Terminate the workflow \\
13 & \hspace*{23.75pt}\textbf{Else:} & \# Restore previous feasibility \\
14 & \hspace*{32.4pt}$\left( \mathcal{C,\ }t \right)\  \leftarrow \ Restore\left( \text{Anchor} \right)$ & \# Recover the latest anchor \\
15 & \hspace*{32.4pt}\textbf{Break} & \# Stop FISTA \\
16 & \hspace*{15.125pt}\textbf{If} KStageEnds: & \# Scheduled or early K update \\
17 & \hspace*{23.75pt}\textbf{If} KStageStable: & \# Three eligible stable comparisons \\
18 & \hspace*{32.4pt}\textbf{Break} & \# Stop catheter selection \\
19 & \hspace*{23.75pt}Update $K$ using Equation (6) & \# Adjust sparsity strength \\
20 & \hspace*{23.75pt}Update weights using Equation (5) & \# Refresh catheter penalties \\
\cmidrule[0.5pt]{2-3}
21 & \textbf{2. Pruning} &  \\
22 & \hspace*{6.475pt}$\tau_{c}\  \leftarrow \ CatheterDwellTotals\left( \mathcal{C,\ }t \right)$ & \# Use the final FISTA solution \\
23 & \hspace*{6.475pt}$Order\  \leftarrow \ SortAscending\left( \mathcal{C,\ }\tau_{c} \right)$ & \# Fix the removal order \\
24 & \hspace*{6.475pt}$Budget\  \leftarrow \ 0.05\sum_{c}^{}\tau_{c};\ Removed\  \leftarrow \ 0$ & \# Fix the deletion budget \\
25 & \hspace*{6.475pt}$t\  \leftarrow \ DoseReopt\left( \mathcal{C,\ }t \right)$ & \# Establish the initial solution \\
26 & \hspace*{6.475pt}\textbf{For} $c$ in Order: & \# Test sequential removal \\
27 & \hspace*{15.125pt}\textbf{If} $\left| \mathcal{C} \right|\  \leq \ 1$ or $Removed\  + \ \tau_{c}\  > \ Budget$: & \# Check removal limits \\
28 & \hspace*{23.75pt}\textbf{Break} & \# Stop Pruning \\
29 & \hspace*{15.125pt}$\mathcal{C}^{\text{trial}}\mathcal{\  \leftarrow \ C\  \smallsetminus \ }\left\{ c \right\}$ & \# Tentatively remove one catheter \\
30 & \hspace*{15.125pt}$t^{\text{trial}}\  \leftarrow \ DoseReopt\left( \mathcal{C}^{\text{trial}},\ t \right)$ & \# Reoptimize remaining dwell times \\
31 & \hspace*{15.125pt}\textbf{If} $F_{\text{dose}}\left( t^{\text{trial}} \right)$ is finite & \# Require a valid dose objective \\
32 & \hspace*{19.45pt}\textbf{and} $D_{90}\left( t^{\text{trial}} \right)\  \geq \ 0.95D_{T}^{\text{LB}}$: & \# Require sufficient coverage \\
33 & \hspace*{23.75pt}$\left( \mathcal{C,\ }t \right)\  \leftarrow \ \left( \mathcal{C}^{\text{trial}},\ t^{\text{trial}} \right)$ & \# Accept the removal \\
34 & \hspace*{23.75pt}$Removed\  \leftarrow \ Removed\  + \ \tau_{c}$ & \# Accumulate removed dwell time \\
35 & \hspace*{15.125pt}\textbf{Else:} & \# Reject the trial configuration \\
36 & \hspace*{23.75pt}\textbf{Break} & \# Retain the last accepted solution \\
37 & \hspace*{6.475pt}\textbf{Return} $\left( \mathcal{C,\ }t \right)$ & \# Pass to configuration optimization \\

\end{tabularx}
\par\vspace{3pt}
\noindent Note: $\mathcal{C}$ denotes the catheter set, and $\left| \mathcal{C} \right|$ its catheter count. $D_{T}^{\text{LB}}$ is the HR-CTV D\textsubscript{90} of the corresponding original, unrescaled clinical plan. DoseReopt minimizes $F_{\text{dose}}\left( t \right)$ using L-BFGS-B with nonnegative bounds and at most 50 iterations. CoverageCheckDue triggers at iteration 551 and subsequently when catheter count falls below that of the latest feasible anchor. KStageEnds and KStageStable follow the update and stability rules in Section 2.2.2 and Appendix A.2. For Pruning, $\tau_{c}$ denotes the total dwell time of catheter $c$ in the final FISTA solution; these values, the ascending removal order, and the 5\% deletion budget remain fixed throughout Pruning. Anchor stores the latest feasible catheter set and FISTA dwell times. The superscripts ``check'' and ``trial'' identify coverage-assessment and tentative-removal variables, respectively.\par
